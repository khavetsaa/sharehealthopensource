# Intended use (draft, for regulatory review)

SHARE has two intended uses.

## 1. Prediction of PPH before birth
SHARE is intended to be used by trained midwives and doctors in health facilities, during labour, to identify women at increased risk of postpartum haemorrhage (PPH) before birth, and to suggest a personalised blood-loss threshold for starting the WHO E-MOTIVE bundle that is never higher than WHO criteria. It is an aid to clinical judgement for screening and risk classification. It is not intended to diagnose PPH.

## 2. Compression during PPH
SHARE is intended to apply external uterine compression after vaginal birth in women diagnosed with PPH and a soft (atonic) uterus, as part of the E-MOTIVE first-response bundle, started and stopped by trained staff.

## SHARE does not
- diagnose PPH or measure blood loss (the E-MOTIVE calibrated drape does)
- replace uterotonics, tranexamic acid, IV fluids, examination or escalation
- act on its own: staff start and stop every action
- get used over a surgical wound

## Expected classification
Kenya Pharmacy and Poisons Board, Class C (our estimate, to be confirmed in a pre-submission meeting).
